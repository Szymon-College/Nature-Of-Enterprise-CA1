# Music and Film
1878-Phonograph
The first case of physical media was around 1878 when the was invented phonograph cylinder where it was the first case of recording media and reproducing media

1889-Vinyl
This used the same idea of the phonograph cylinder where instead of a waxed/metal cylinder it used a disc shape, it used a needle liked the phonograph which would moved on the grove of the vinyl to reproduce the sound. The vinyl Became the main use to record and listen to sound. 

1963-Cassette 
Cassette are magnetic analog tapes , this made physical media more consumer friendly as they were more sturdy and less bulky than Vinyl. Cassettes fit easily into pockets, bags, car stereos making consumers in which the vinyl could not , being more than a fixed location. 

1970's-90's 

The first cases of physical media  for film were Vhs and Betamax , these formats used magnetic tape to record and show film.

1979-Walkman Sony brought out the Walkman which used cassettes to record and play sounds in a small devices, this was a stand out for psychical media in music as it helped bring Cassettes to more homes across the world and outside the home with it's portability 

1982-present-CDs 
Sony and Phillips brought out a the compact disc (CD), It's main purpose was to unify digital audio and film formats. It read music and film using a laser instead of a needle like a vinyl or tape rolling like a cassette , it brought much better audio quality than previous formats and proper switching ,rewind and sound skipping without gradual sound degradation associated with repeated cassette playback.

This has been the way for physical media formats with future versions (MiniDisc , DVD etc) sharing the same design with each version improving sound quality and higher storage captivity 

1998-Digital audio formats 
This marked the major turning point to physical to the modern day streaming. First in 1998 a new format called MPEG Audio Layer III (MP3) which compressed music files, this meant that audio files could be played and stored on computers and the possibility of transforming  the file over the internet , this marked the beginning of physical to digital music 

1999–2001 -Music File Sharing
Napster was launched as an online music-sharing service, allowing users to share and download MP3 files directly from one another. this marked the first threat for physical media as Napster was a major success with it being a Illegal way to download music over the internet .

services Apple's iTunes Music Store in 2003 came out which was a legal online music-sharing service backed by music labels, this made music labels happy as they had a way to earn money over the internet but threatened physical media with it being more convenient than physical.

In 2008, Spotify launched with it being rather than downloading individual songs or purchasing physical albums, consumers could access a large online music library and play tracks whenever they wanted. Steaming now was the main income of music with physical audio formats sales falling every year.
