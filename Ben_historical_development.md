# Music 
1878-Phonograph
The first case of physical media was around 1878 when the was invented phonograph cylinder where it was the first case of recording media and reproducing media, it worked using a waxed cylinder and with a stylus would etch waveforms into the cylinder  when played with a needle moved by a vibrating membrane would generate sound that was recorded, The material changed from wax to metal to help persevere after long use

1889-Vinyl
This used the same idea of the phonograph cylinder where instead of a waxed/metal cylinder it used a disc shape, it used a needle liked the phonograph which would moved on the grove of the vinyl to reproduce the sound. The vinyl Became the main use to record and listen to sound. 

1963-Cassette 
Cassette are magnetic analog tapes , this made physical media more consumer friendly as they were more sturdy and less bulky than Vinyl. Cassettes fit easily into pockets, bags, car stereos making consumers in which the vinyl could not , being more than a fixed location. 

1979-Walkman Sony brought out the Walkman which used cassettes to record and play sounds in a small devices, this was a stand out for psychical media in music as it helped bring Cassettes to more homes across the world and outside the home with it's portability 

1982-present-CDs and other discs psychical media
Sony and Phillips brought out a the compact disc (CD), It's main purpose was to unify digital audio formats. It read music using a laser instead of a needle like a vinyl or tape rolling like a cassette , it brought much better audio quality than previous formats and proper switching ,rewind and sound skipping without gradual sound degradation associated with repeated cassette playback.

This has been the way for physical media audio formats with future versions (MiniDisc , DVD and blue-ray) sharing the same design with each version improving sound quality and higher storage captivity
